## 🔬 About Me

I am a PhD research fellow passionate about unraveling the molecular mechanisms of bacterial communication, gene regulation, and mutagenesis. My research bridges **experimental molecular biology** with **computational genomics & bioinformatics** to understand microbial adaptation and antimicrobial resistance (AMR) dissemination.

- 🎓 **PhD Candidate** in molecular microbiology and bioinformatics
- 🦠 Investigating the molecular mechanisms of **competence regulation** and **natural genetic transformation** in oral *Streptococci*
- 💻 Developing automated computational workflows and pipelines to accelerate *in vitro* discovery
- 🧪 Bridging wet-lab phenotypic assays with large-scale genomic data analysis

---

## 🧬 Research Focus

| Focus | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| 🧫 **Bacterial Signaling** | Quorum sensing and peptide-mediated communication in microbial communities |
| 🧬 **Genetic Transformation** | Molecular machinery of horizontal gene transfer and exogenous DNA uptake |
| 🧪 **Mutagenesis** | Mutation pathways, spontaneous rates, and adaptation under selective pressure |
| 💊 **Antimicrobial Resistance** | Quantifying the relative contributions of transformation vs. mutagenesis in AMR propagation |
| 🦷 **Oral *Streptococci*** | Commensal and opportunistic pathobionts exhibiting high adaptability and clinical relevance |

<br>

<details>
  <summary>🔬 <b>Deep Dive: Experimental &amp; Wet-Lab Methodologies</b></summary>
  <br>

  - **Natural Transformation &amp; Competence Induction:** Quantitative transformation frequency assays, competence-stimulating peptide (CSP / XIP) response profiling, and targeted homologous recombination in *Streptococcus constellatus*.
  - **Mutagenesis &amp; Fluctuation Analysis:** Luria–Delbrück fluctuation assays to determine spontaneous mutation rates and evaluate mutational spectra during antibiotic exposure.
  - **In Vitro Toxicology &amp; Co-culture Models:** Evaluating innate immune responses (human alveolar cell co-cultures, neutrophil-like HL-60 models) exposed to micro/nanoplastics and microbial contaminants.
  - **Antimicrobial Susceptibility Testing (AST):** High-resolution growth curve kinetics, minimum inhibitory concentration (MIC) assays, and population survival dynamics.
</details>

<details>
  <summary>💻 <b>Deep Dive: Bioinformatics &amp; Computational Genomics</b></summary>
  <br>

  - **Reproducible NGS Pipelines:** Developing automated, containerized pipelines using [Nextflow](https://www.nextflow.io/) adhering to [nf-core](https://nf-co.re/) standards for whole-genome sequencing and variant calling.
  - **Biosynthetic Gene Cluster (BGC) Mining:** Mining and comparative exploration of secondary metabolite clusters using antiSMASH, GECCO, DeepBGC, and BiG-SCAPE (e.g., via [Clystere](https://github.com/andreassag/clystere)).
  - **AMR Determinant Mapping:** Resistome annotation, mobile genetic element (MGE) tracking, and core- vs. accessory-genome alignment across clinical and commensal isolates.
  - **Data Science &amp; Tooling:** Custom Python/R utilities for batch microplate normalization, statistical modeling, and image processing workflows.
</details>
